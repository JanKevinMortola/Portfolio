Choosing the right automation platform

I do not treat n8n, Make, Zapier, and GoHighLevel as interchangeable. Zapier can be ideal for fast SaaS-to-SaaS automations, Make is useful for visual multi-step scenarios and data handling, GoHighLevel combines CRM and marketing workflows, while n8n provides strong flexibility for APIs, custom logic, self-hosted setups, and more technical orchestration. The best choice depends on the business process, maintenance requirements, budget, and complexity.

CRM should connect to operations

A CRM is most valuable when sales and customer information can trigger the next operational action. I design systems where a qualified lead, booked appointment, won opportunity, client update, or service event can flow into the correct downstream workflow without unnecessary duplicate entry.
