Case Study · Hospitality Operations

Automation for Hospitality & Short-Term Rental Operations

My hospitality background gives me a different starting point from a purely technical automation developer: I understand that guest communication, reservation data, cleaning operations, property readiness, exceptions, and staff handoffs all affect one another. The automation has to support the operating reality, not just move data between apps.

Typical operational challenges

Short-term rental and hospitality teams often work across a property management system, messaging tools, spreadsheets, pricing platforms, cleaning processes, and manual staff communication. Problems appear when reservation identifiers are missing, guest data does not match cleanly, a complaint is already open, or a workflow sends a message without understanding the current operational state.

Automation patterns I design

Reservation and guest-data matching

When a form or external source does not include a reliable reservation ID, I design matching logic around the available signals—such as guest name, property, dates, email, phone, or arrival information—and route low-confidence matches for manual review rather than forcing an unsafe automatic match.

Guest communication with exception awareness

Scheduled messages should not blindly fire when a guest has an unresolved complaint or unusual reservation state. I use conditional logic so workflows can pause, branch, escalate, or request human review when the situation needs context.

Cleaning and turnover operations

Reservation events can be transformed into operational tasks for cleaning, readiness, status tracking, and staff notification. The goal is to reduce manual coordination while still keeping exceptions visible.

Spreadsheet and API bridges

For smaller portfolios, a carefully designed Google Sheet or Apps Script layer can sometimes be more maintainable than adding another paid platform. Where stronger orchestration is needed, n8n can connect APIs, webhooks, validation, notifications, and operational data into one workflow.
