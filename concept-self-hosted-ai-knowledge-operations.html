CONCEPT PROJECT · DEMO ARCHITECTURE

Self-Hosted AI Knowledge & Operations Assistant

This concept demonstrates a privacy-conscious AI assistant that can answer questions from approved company knowledge, summarize operational data, and propose workflow actions while keeping sensitive actions behind human approval.

Business scenario

A company has SOPs, project documentation, client records, policies, and operational data spread across Drive, ClickUp, spreadsheets, and internal notes. Staff repeatedly search for answers or ask managers for information that already exists.

Architecture

  • Local model layer: self-hosted model served through Ollama or LM Studio for selected workloads.
  • Retrieval layer: approved documents are chunked, indexed, and retrieved with source metadata and permission boundaries.
  • n8n orchestration: handles queries, retrieval, tool calls, routing, logging, and approval workflows.
  • Business tools: read-only connections can surface context from ClickUp, Google Drive, Sheets, or internal APIs.
  • Action layer: proposed writes such as creating tasks, sending messages, or changing records require policy checks and, where appropriate, human approval.

Example capabilities

  • Answer “What is our process for this exception?” with source-linked SOP context
  • Summarize unresolved operational issues from ClickUp
  • Draft an action plan from current project status
  • Detect conflicting or outdated documentation and flag it for review
  • Prepare a task or message for approval without sending automatically

Governance and reliability

  • Source citation required for knowledge answers
  • Permission-aware retrieval
  • Read/write tools separated by policy
  • Prompt and tool-call audit trail
  • Fallback to human review when confidence or retrieval quality is low
  • Evaluation set for hallucination, permission leakage, and stale-document behavior
