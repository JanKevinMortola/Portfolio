CONCEPT PROJECT · DEMO ARCHITECTURE

AI Lead Qualification & CRM Revenue Engine

This is a portfolio concept project designed to demonstrate how I would architect a high-complexity lead-to-client automation system. It is not presented as completed client work.

Business scenario

A service business receives leads from website forms, paid campaigns, social channels, and referral partners. Sales needs fast qualification, consistent follow-up, clean CRM data, appointment routing, and automatic handoff to operations when a deal closes.

Architecture

Capture: Forms / ads / chat → webhook intake

Normalize: n8n validates fields, deduplicates contacts, enriches context, and creates a canonical lead record.

AI qualification: AI classifies intent, urgency, service fit, and missing information using a controlled scoring rubric.

CRM: GoHighLevel creates/updates contact and opportunity records, assigns pipeline stage, tags, owner, and follow-up sequence.

Routing: Make or Zapier handles fast SaaS handoffs where appropriate; n8n retains the complex orchestration and exception logic.

Conversion: Booked or won opportunities trigger ClickUp delivery tasks, Google Workspace folders, onboarding messages, and internal notifications.

Failure handling

  • Duplicate lead detection using email, phone, source, and fuzzy name matching
  • Manual review queue for low-confidence AI classifications
  • Retry logic for CRM/API failures with idempotency keys
  • Dead-letter logging for malformed payloads
  • Owner escalation when a hot lead has no activity within a defined SLA

Why this is complex

The challenge is not just moving leads between apps. The architecture must preserve one source of truth, prevent duplicates, manage confidence and human review, survive partial failures, and keep sales and delivery systems synchronized.
