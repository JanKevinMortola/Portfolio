CONCEPT PROJECT · DEMO ARCHITECTURE

Recruitment, Interview & Employee Onboarding Automation

This concept project demonstrates how I would design an end-to-end candidate pipeline that combines automation, AI-assisted screening, scheduling, human approvals, document workflows, and operational onboarding.

Business scenario

A remote company hires across multiple roles and receives applications from several channels. Recruiters need consistent screening, interview coordination, scorecards, offer approvals, document collection, account provisioning checklists, and onboarding visibility without copying the same data repeatedly.

Workflow architecture

  1. Application form or recruiting source creates a canonical candidate record.
  2. n8n validates required fields, deduplicates candidates, extracts resume details, and maps them into structured fields.
  3. AI generates a role-specific screening summary against a defined rubric, but does not make the final hiring decision.
  4. Qualified candidates enter a CRM/pipeline stage and receive interview scheduling options.
  5. Calendar events trigger interviewer reminders, scorecard tasks, and follow-up status checks.
  6. Approved candidates trigger offer/document generation and approval routing.
  7. Accepted offers create ClickUp onboarding projects, Google Drive folders, training tasks, access checklists, and first-week milestones.

Governance

  • Human review required for screening disposition and hiring decisions
  • AI output stored as advisory context, not an automatic acceptance/rejection action
  • Role-specific scoring rubrics versioned and auditable
  • PII access limited by workflow role and destination
  • Exception queue for missing documents, scheduling conflicts, and stale candidates
