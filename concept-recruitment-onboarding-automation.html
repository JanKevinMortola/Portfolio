Concept mockup: demonstrates the proposed workflow while preserving human accountability.

Candidate lifecycle

Move information once, then reuse it.

The architecture treats candidate identity, assessment, interview state and onboarding status as connected records rather than repeating data across email threads and spreadsheets.

Structured resume extraction

Convert documents into normalized experience, skills and candidate data while preserving the original source for review.

Evidence-based AI assist

AI summarizes evidence against role criteria but does not become the sole hiring decision-maker.

Interview orchestration

Create scheduling actions, reminders and evaluation tasks from candidate stage changes.

Approval workflow

Require explicit human decisions before offers, rejections or sensitive downstream actions.

Onboarding handoff

Once hired, create the operational onboarding project, document checklist and team responsibilities automatically.

Exception visibility

Missing documents, conflicting candidate data and stalled approvals enter a visible queue rather than disappearing in email.

Design principle

Automate administration—not accountability.

AI and workflow automation can reduce repetitive screening and coordination work while preserving human judgment for consequential employment decisions.
