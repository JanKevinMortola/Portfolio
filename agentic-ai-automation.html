Agentic AI & Self-Hosted AI Automation

I explore and build AI workflows that can reason over business context, use approved tools, carry out controlled actions, and hand decisions back to people when human review is required. My focus is not on adding AI everywhere; it is on finding where AI can improve an existing operating process without making the system harder to trust.

Where agentic AI can help

Useful applications include triaging operational requests, classifying incoming information, preparing structured drafts, extracting data, choosing between approved workflow branches, coordinating tasks across tools, summarizing exceptions, and assisting operators with decisions based on defined business rules and context.

Why governance matters

AI systems should have clear boundaries. I design around permissions, approved tools, human approval points, fallback behavior, traceable outputs, usage controls, and the difference between an AI recommendation and an action that is safe to execute automatically.

Self-hosted and local AI

For workflows where cost, privacy, experimentation, or infrastructure control matter, I also work with local and self-hosted model setups. These can be combined with automation platforms and APIs so a business can evaluate where local inference is practical and where a hosted model is still the better choice.

AI should fit the operating system

My broader work includes n8n, ClickUp, Google Apps Script, APIs, workflow architecture, reporting, and operations design. That lets me treat AI as one component inside a governed business workflow rather than as a disconnected chatbot.
