Concept Project · Customer Operations

E-commerce AI Support & Order Operations.

A portfolio architecture for combining AI support triage with order-system context, policy checks, risk-based routing, human approval and auditable operational actions.

Concept architecture: this is a demonstration project, not a claimed client implementation.
AIn8nMakeZapierCRM / HelpdeskAPIs
Decision architecture

Automate the low-risk path. Escalate the rest.

The system separates language understanding from policy and authorization so AI cannot independently make every customer-impacting decision.

Customer MessageEmail · chat · ticket · form
→

→
Order ContextOrder · shipment · payment · history

Policy GateRisk · thresholds · authorization
→
Resolve / EscalateAction · human review · audit log

Structured AI triage

Convert free-text tickets into normalized intent, urgency, order references and recommended next actions.

Live order context

Pull current order, shipment and customer history before deciding whether the request is simple or exceptional.

Risk-based routing

Automatically handle low-risk informational requests while routing refunds, unusual claims or conflicting data for human review.

Human approval

Sensitive actions require explicit approval before the workflow changes money, customer status or fulfillment state.

Exception queue

Missing order IDs, inconsistent data and failed API calls remain visible with enough context for a person to resolve them.

Audit trail

Store the request, context, AI output, policy result, action and reviewer decision for later analysis.

Design principle

AI understands language; policy controls consequences.

Separating those two layers makes customer-service automation more useful, testable and safer to operate.
