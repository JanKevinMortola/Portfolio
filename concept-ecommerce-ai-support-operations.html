Structured AI triage

Convert free-text tickets into normalized intent, urgency, order references and recommended next actions.

Live order context

Pull current order, shipment and customer history before deciding whether the request is simple or exceptional.

Risk-based routing

Handle low-risk informational requests while routing sensitive cases for human review.

Human approval

Sensitive actions require explicit approval before consequential changes.

Exception queue

Missing IDs, conflicting data and failed API calls remain visible for resolution.

Audit trail

Store the request, context, AI output, policy result, action and reviewer decision.

Design principle

AI understands language; policy controls consequences.

Separating those two layers makes customer-service automation more useful, testable and safer to operate.
