E-commerce AI Support & Order Operations Control Layer

This concept demonstrates a controlled AI-assisted workflow for customer support, order status, refund/return routing, fulfillment exceptions, and escalation without allowing AI to take high-risk actions autonomously.

Business scenario

A growing online store receives high volumes of repetitive questions alongside sensitive cases involving refunds, missing deliveries, damaged orders, cancellations, and VIP customers. Support agents spend time searching order data across systems before they can respond.

Architecture

  • Support inbox/form/chat events enter through webhook or automation connector.
  • n8n resolves the customer and order from commerce/CRM data.
  • AI classifies intent and extracts order number, issue type, urgency, sentiment, and requested resolution.
  • Policy engine determines whether the case can receive an automated informational response or requires human approval.
  • Make/Zapier handles selected SaaS notifications and ticket syncs.
  • Complex exceptions create structured ClickUp tasks with customer/order context and SLA timers.

Decision controls

  • Low risk: tracking status, policy explanation, FAQ, delivery estimate → draft/auto response depending on policy.
  • Medium risk: return eligibility, address issue, reship request → agent review.
  • High risk: refund, charge dispute, fraud signal, account security → mandatory human approval.

Reliability design

  • Confidence threshold before AI classification is accepted
  • Order lookup fallback using email + name + date when ID is missing
  • Audit log of model output, rules, and final human action
  • Duplicate ticket detection
  • Escalation for high-value customers or repeated delivery failures
