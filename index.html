My automation standard

Validate → Automate → Verify → Recover.

A workflow is not production-ready just because the happy path runs. I design what happens before the write, after the write and when the expected state does not happen.

ValidateIdentity, required data and state.
AutomateExplicit routing and ownership.
VerifyConfirm the destination changed.
RecoverSurface failures for action.
