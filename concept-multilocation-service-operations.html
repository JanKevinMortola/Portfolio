Branch-aware routing

Assign service work based on geography, availability, service type or custom operational constraints.

Capacity-aware scheduling

Prevent over-assignment by checking team capacity and existing schedule context before confirming delivery.

Work-order state machine

Move jobs through scheduled, assigned, en route, in progress, completed and exception states with explicit ownership.

SLA monitoring

Escalate unassigned, overdue or blocked jobs before they become customer-impacting failures.

Reconciliation

Compare planned service, delivered work, completion evidence and billing state before closing the operational cycle.

Management view

Roll up branch volume, backlog, exceptions and service performance without requiring managers to inspect every work order.

Design principle

Scale the operating model, not just the automation count.

Shared state definitions and explicit exception handling keep automation understandable as the business grows.
