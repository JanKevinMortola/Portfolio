Multi-Location Service Operations Command System

This portfolio concept demonstrates how I would connect CRM, field operations, scheduling, customer communication, quality control, and management reporting for a growing service company operating across multiple locations.

Business scenario

A company operates several service territories. Leads enter centrally, but scheduling, staff assignment, service delivery, exceptions, customer updates, and reporting vary by branch. Leadership needs one operating picture without forcing every team to work inside the same technical system.

System design

  • GoHighLevel: contacts, opportunities, customer communications, appointment events, pipeline visibility.
  • ClickUp: operational work orders, branch queues, assignments, checklists, escalations, and management views.
  • n8n: central orchestration, data normalization, branch routing, SLA checks, reconciliation, and exception workflows.
  • Make/Zapier: lighter integrations for branch-specific SaaS tools and rapid trigger/action flows.
  • Google Workspace: supporting files, Sheets-based imports, summaries, and exception reports.

Workflow

  1. Won CRM opportunity creates an operational service order.
  2. Rules determine branch, service type, required skills, duration, priority, and due window.
  3. ClickUp task is generated with customer context and linked CRM identifiers.
  4. Assignment workflow checks staff capacity and flags schedule conflicts.
  5. Service completion triggers customer follow-up, QA checks, invoice-ready status, and management metrics.
  6. Failed or delayed jobs move into an exception queue with escalation timers.

Controls and resilience

  • Idempotent create/update logic prevents duplicate work orders
  • Branch-specific routing table avoids hard-coded workflow duplication
  • SLA monitor detects unassigned, overdue, and stalled jobs
  • Reconciliation job compares CRM and operations records for mismatch
  • Human override path for reassignment and priority escalation
