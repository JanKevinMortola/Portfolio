My systems approach

I start with the source of truth, data relationships, failure modes, exception states, human approvals, and measurable outputs before selecting a platform. That lets me use n8n, Make, Zapier, GoHighLevel, ClickUp, Google Workspace, APIs, or AI for the parts they are best suited to instead of forcing the entire business into one tool.
